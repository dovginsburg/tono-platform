## Social captions

**Launch / hero**
> four ways to say the same thing. tono rewrites your draft into calmer, warmer, sharper, or shorter — you pick, you copy, you send. 14-day free trial, no free-tier games. tonoit.com

**Product**
> paste a draft. get four tones back in a second. keep the one that sounds like you. tono lives on your keyboard and in your browser — your words never leave without you hitting rewrite.

**Pricing**
> tono pro: $3.99/mo or $39.99/yr after a 14-day free trial. one account, cancel anytime in the portal, no dark patterns. rewrites fail closed when the trial ends — no bait-and-switch.

**Auth / passkeys**
> sign in the way you want: google, apple, magic link — or a passkey (face id / touch id / windows hello). phishing-resistant, password-free, and you can manage or revoke them from your account.

**Mobile**
> tono on your phone, in the flow of the message you're already writing. same four tones, same one-tap copy, tuned for the thumb.
